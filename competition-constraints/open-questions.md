## Budget arithmetic (from Q3 + Q7)

The run is sequential, about 120 tasks, with a hard 12-hour cap on the whole job. Leave about 30 minutes for vLLM start-up and per-task container setup (about 15 s × 120). That leaves roughly 11.5 h ÷ 120 ≈ **5.5 minutes per task at most**. A safe setting is `max_time_minutes: 5` with `timeout_seconds` ≤ 120. Your `AgenticSubmission/v4_semdag` uses 8 minutes, which is about 16 hours worst case and **would error the whole submission**. v1, v2 and v3 use 5 minutes, which is fine.
